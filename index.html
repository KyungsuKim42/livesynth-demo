LiveSynth

Playable Generative Neural Synthesis with Flexible Timbre Control

Kyungsu Kim, Yejin Kim, and Kyogu Lee

Music and Audio Research Group, Seoul National University

LiveSynth generates audio from incoming MIDI events with timbre specified by a reference recording or text prompt. It operates causally at a 100-Hz frame rate and supports timbre morphing and autonomous continuation.
