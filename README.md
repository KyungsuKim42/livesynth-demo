# LiveSynth Audio Demo

Audio demonstrations for **LiveSynth: A Streaming Neural Synthesizer for Instrument Cloning and Text-to-Instrument** by Kyungsu Kim, Yejin Kim, and Kyogu Lee at the Music and Audio Research Group, Seoul National University.

The page includes examples of zero-shot instrument cloning, Text-to-Instrument, timbre morphing, autonomous performance continuation, and real-time inference.
